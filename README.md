# My-Money-PWA

**My-Money-PWA** is a secure Progressive Web App designed to help you track your expenses and save money effectively.

## 🔒 Privacy & Security First
* **No Bank Access**: This app **does not** take or access any of your bank details, account credentials, or UPI details.
* **No Third-Party Linking**: It does not connect to or interact with any bank accounts or UPI apps. Your financial data stays safe, private, and entirely under your control.

## ✨ Features
* **Expense Tracking**: Easily monitor where your money goes to help you save more.
* **Fast & Lightweight**: Works smoothly as a Progressive Web App (PWA) across mobile and desktop devices.
* **Offline Ready**: Access your data anytime, even without an internet connection.

## 🚀 Getting Started
1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open the application in your browser and start tracking your finances securely.
